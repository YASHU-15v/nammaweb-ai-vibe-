What you're actually learning

Two skills, one program: vibe coding and agentic AI.

Most courses teach syntax. This one teaches you to work the way AI-native teams already do — and BE, BTech and BCA students are exactly who we built it for.

Vibe coding

You describe what you want to build in plain English, and iterate with an AI pair programmer until it works — reading the output, correcting its assumptions, and shaping it into something real. It's less about memorising syntax and more about knowing what good software looks like.

Agentic AI

One prompt, one answer isn't enough for real work. Agentic AI means designing systems that plan their own steps, call tools, check their own output, and complete multi-step tasks with little supervision — the skill employers are actively hiring for right now.

How the program is structured

Four phases. Every track runs through them — the longer tracks just go deeper.

No phase is a lecture series. Each one ends with something you built, not a slide you sat through.
